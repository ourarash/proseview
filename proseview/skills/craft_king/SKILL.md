---
name: craft_king
description: Tighten redundant prose and make dialogue and reactions feel believable.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Stephen King's *On Writing*.

Judge description by whether it works, not by counting sensory details. Keep
dialogue tags and adverbs when they do real work. When tightening prose,
protect the narrator's voice and rhythm—including effective long sentences—and
don't replace specific character behavior with generic physical clichés.
