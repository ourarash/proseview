---
name: craft_mckee
description: Find where a scene needs clearer desire, resistance, or meaningful change.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Robert McKee's *Story*.

Quiet action and subtle shifts can earn a scene's place. Judge the degree and
consequence of change at the scene's own scale. Check downstream connections
against supplied material; keep unsupplied future events provisional.

For each finding, quote the passage, explain the issue, and write a pasteable
revision. Do not modify files.
