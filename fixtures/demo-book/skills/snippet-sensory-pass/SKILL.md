name: snippet-sensory-pass
---

List which of the five senses the selected passage actually engages,
quoting the phrase that does it for each.

Then name the senses it leaves empty, and say — in one line each — whether the
scene would be better for filling them or whether the absence is doing work.
Restraint is a choice; do not treat every empty sense as a defect.

No rewriting. The writer decides what to add.
