# Demo Novel Plan

## Act One

- Chapter 1: Rena cannot open her safe; the week is already failing.
- Chapter 2: Patel arrives with a partnership offer and a deadline.

## Act Two

- Chapter 3: Rena tries to prove the offer is predatory and fails.
- Chapter 4: Rena accepts a smaller, worse offer to avoid the larger one.

## Act Three

- Chapter 5: The worse offer reveals itself as the trap Rena was trying to dodge.
- Chapter 6: Rena chooses to lose the shop cleanly instead of keeping it dirty.
