Plain files, no lock-in

Your book stays a plain folder on disk.

Proseview is 100% free and MIT open source, with no paid tier, no account, and no server storing your manuscript. Point it at a folder of chapters and scenes—it reads your Markdown directly and leaves every file yours forever.

Edit in place, search everywhere

Press E to write in the same typography you read in, edit story bibles and YAML notes in place, and search the entire manuscript with Cmd/Ctrl+K across six themes and seven fonts.

Progress, streaks, and what changed

See words written toward your goal, daily pace, streak, and the exact words added or cut in every scene—computed directly from your local files and Git history.

Works with your tools

Fits the way you already write.

Obsidian

Point Proseview at your vault and nothing moves. Wikilinks, callouts, and embeds stay exactly as you wrote them.

Git, for backup

Your book is plain text, so Git keeps every draft. Push it to a private GitHub repository for off-site backup, and Proseview reads your commits for goals and writing streaks.

VS Code

Open any scene in VS Code, Cursor, or Zed with one click and keep it beside Proseview. A change saved in either one appears live in the other.

Revise & plan · no AI

Instant prose checks, on your own terms.

Catch weak phrasing while you revise without sending a word off your machine. Switch on any of 13 local style passes and watch matching passages underline quietly in your prose.

  • 13 local style passes: repetition, echoes, filter verbs, passive voice, crutch words, dialogue tags, POV slips, and more.
  • Plain-English takeaways: pacing outliers, vocabulary variety (MATTR & MTLD), and a chapter-by-chapter character chart.

Structure

See every storyline at once.

Spot chapters that run long and trace every plot thread across the entire novel. Tag scenes with thread: or a color label like Main Plot or Flashback, and watch the whole book's rhythm snap into view.

Scene reviews

Review notes you can preview and apply.

Run a quick critique, deepen and layer, comedy review, or craft review through McKee's Story, The Story Grid, Story Genius, Save the Cat!, On Writing, or Bird by Bird. Every note quotes the passage, explains why it falters, and offers a rewrite in the scene's own voice.

  • Compare side by sideInspect any suggestion against your current draft before changing a word.
  • Pick & steerCheck the notes you want, add your own guidance, and apply them together.
  • Reversible by defaultEvery agent edit saves with a diff card and one-click Undo all.

Pacing & tension

Beat Map: see how a scene moves.

Plot an estimated tension curve across the top of your scene or in the side panel alongside every beat in order—dialogue, action, internal thought, and silent reaction—without touching your prose. Step through moments to highlight each passage in place; Proseview flags the map out of date whenever the scene changes.

Ask & edit across the whole book

Three side-by-side agent tabs that search the entire manuscript, read your story notes, edit multiple files at once, and show exact context and token usage for every reply.

The Group: let your agents debate

Put two or three agents in one conversation—focused on story, voice, and continuity—to push back on each other and summarize agreed Before/After edits, or forward any chat for a second opinion.

Illustrations

A picture for every scene.

Ask Codex, Antigravity, or Claude for an illustration in six styles, from woodcut to oil painting. Previews wait above the scene until you insert them, then save beside the chapter as ordinary image links so Obsidian, VS Code, and GitHub show them too.

Publish

Export ready-to-publish books.

Move straight from Markdown scenes to a finished book—with plain-language preflight checks and Typst bundled inside, so there is no Pandoc, LaTeX, or Calibre to install.

  • Paperback PDF & EPUB3: Typeset interiors from 5 × 8 to 6 × 9 inches or A5 with facing-page preview, plus store-ready EPUB.
  • Word & submissions: Export a .docx for your editor's tracked changes or standard manuscript format for literary agents.

Remote mode

Read, edit, and dictate from your phone.

Run proseview --remote to open your book on your phone over your private Tailscale network—no extra accounts, passwords, or pairing. Read continuously, tap a paragraph to leave a TODO, dictate a quick edit, or ask an agent about the scene.

  • Safe sync: Drafts stay on your phone until saved, with conflict protection so computer and phone edits never silently overwrite each other.

Get started

Install Proseview.

On your computer

macOS, Windows, and Linux · nothing to install first

Open Terminal (on a Mac, press Cmd-Space and type Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

curl -LsSf https://ourarash.github.io/proseview/install.sh | sh

Open PowerShell (press the Windows key and type PowerShell), paste this line and press Enter:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ourarash/proseview/main/site/install.ps1 | iex
  • It asks where your book is: press Enter to start a new one, or drag in your book's folder.
  • It adds a Proseview launcher (on your Desktop on macOS and Windows, or in your apps menu on Linux). Open it anytime to launch your book; your files stay on your computer.
  • Run the same line again to update.

Already use Python? pipx install proseview (or pipx install "proseview[claude]" for the Claude tab), then run proseview in your book's folder.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Proseview is open source under the MIT license, with no paid version, no subscription, and no account. If you use the optional AI panel, Codex, Claude, or Antigravity bill you under your own plan with them.

Where is my book stored?

On your computer, as plain Markdown files in your own folder. If you start a book in the browser, it is kept in that browser's storage on your device, and clearing the site's data deletes it, so use Download it to keep a copy. Proseview has no server that receives your book. To back it up, keep the folder in Git and push it to a private repository, on GitHub or anywhere else.

Do I need Obsidian?

No. Any folder of Markdown files works, and Proseview can create the files for you. If you do use Obsidian, Proseview works on your vault without changing how it is written.

Do I need Git?

No. Proseview works on any folder. Git is the easiest way to back the book up and keep every draft, and in a Git folder Proseview also shows your goals: today's words, your pace, the writing streak, and what changed.

Do I need AI?

No. Everything except the agent panel works without it, and the agent panel does nothing until you ask it something.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes. The sample book shows the dashboard for Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, and you can start a book in your browser with nothing to install. A book started there stays in that browser until you download it.

Can I move a book from the browser to my computer?

Yes. Download it gives you the book as a zip of its folder. Unzip it and run proseview open path/to/folder.
