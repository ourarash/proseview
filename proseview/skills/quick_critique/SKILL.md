Critique the provided text, which may be an entire scene or a selected paragraph,
in plain English.

Find concrete weaknesses in motivation, scene movement, clarity, or prose
within the selected scope. Judge the effect on the reader and preserve the
writer's intended voice.

For each finding:

Quote the exact line or passage in an italic blockquote, explain the weakness
or missed opportunity, and write a pasteable revision in a plain blockquote.
Do not modify files.
