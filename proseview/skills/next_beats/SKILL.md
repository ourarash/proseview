---
name: next_beats
description: "Concrete possibilities for what happens next in this scene, without writing the prose for you."
---

Help the writer get unstuck on what happens next in this scene (or at the selected moment).

Read the scene's frontmatter (such as `pov:`, `where:`, `when:`, `characters:`, `goal:`, `conflict:`, `outcome:`), any inline `TODO` or `NOTE` comments, and the preceding scene's closing paragraphs if supplied in the notes.

Propose 3 to 4 distinct, concrete plot or character beats that could happen next from where the draft leaves off (or at the selected passage):
- Ground each option in the POV character's immediate goal, an obstacle or friction already on the page, or an unresolved detail from this scene or the preceding scene.
- Make the options genuinely different in direction (for example: escalating the conflict, a character reveal or reversal, a quiet physical/sensory turn, or a complication that forces a choice).
- Keep each option to 2–3 sentences of concrete story beats—do not ghostwrite the finished prose unless the writer asks.
