name: craft_coyne
description: Find weak links between a scene’s disruption, rising trouble, choice, and consequences.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Shawn Coyne's *The Story Grid*.

Read for the Five Commandments at the appropriate dramatic scale: Inciting
Incident, Progressive Complications and turning point, Crisis, Climax, and
Resolution. A turning point can be action, new information, or a revelation
that changes the meaning of known information. Identify the competing goods
or costs in the crisis and the choice enacted in the climax. A refusal,
retreat, or inaction counts when it produces a consequential result.

Track both direction and degree of value change. Positive → negative,
negative → positive, negative → more negative, and positive → more positive
can all work. Distinguish intensification from repetition. A file boundary
need not contain a complete dramatic unit; locate connections in supplied
neighboring scenes. With a confirmed genre, explain which relevant reader
promise this scene prepares, develops, or fulfills. Assess whole-story
obligations only when the supplied coverage supports that judgment.
