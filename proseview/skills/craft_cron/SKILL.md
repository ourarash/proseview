---
name: craft_cron
description: Make the connection between events, personal meaning, and character choices clearer.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Lisa Cron's *Story Genius*.

Distinguish writer-supplied beliefs from what readers can infer in the prose.
Sensible choices and restraint can reveal character. Do not diagnose a
formative wound or core misbelief from an ambiguous gesture; missing history
limits that diagnosis rather than proving a defect.

For each finding, quote the passage, explain the issue, and write a pasteable
revision. Do not modify files.
