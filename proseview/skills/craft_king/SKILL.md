---
name: craft_king
description: Tighten redundant prose and make dialogue and reactions feel believable.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Stephen King's *On Writing*.

Examine believable behavior, distinct dialogue, selective description,
background, and language. Do reactions follow the established person and
situation? Do speakers sound like particular people through what they notice,
avoid, and say, rather than everyone sharing the same phrasing? Identify
redundant explanatory tags and adverbs while retaining useful attribution
and modifiers that change meaning.

Check whether a few well-chosen physical details let the reader see the scene
and imagine the rest. Identify a concrete visualization problem or an excess
of description by its effect, without imposing a sensory quota. Ask whether
backstory serves the present moment. Examine redundant qualifiers, strained
images, and sentence and paragraph rhythm. Preserve deliberate voice and
rhythm; tightening should not make every sentence short or replace each
character's behavior with stock gestures.

Then fix what you find: each problem ends in text the writer can paste.
