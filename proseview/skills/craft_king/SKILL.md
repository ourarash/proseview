---
name: craft_king
description: Tighten redundant prose and make dialogue and reactions feel believable.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Stephen King's *On Writing*.

Judge description by its effect without a sensory quota. Retain useful
attribution and modifiers. Tightening should preserve deliberate voice and
rhythm, including effective long sentences, and keep characters' behavior
particular rather than replacing it with stock gestures.

For each finding, quote the passage, explain the issue, and write a pasteable
revision. Do not modify files.
