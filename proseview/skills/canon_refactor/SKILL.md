---
name: canon_refactor
description: After a story fact changes, find passages and consequences that may need updating.
---

Trace the writer's requested change to an established story fact through the
supplied manuscript and reference material. Restate the old fact and intended
new fact in the summary. If the request leaves a key fact undecided, identify
that decision instead of choosing an answer for the writer.

Look beyond matching names or words. Check consequences for dates and ages,
locations, relationships, object history, causality, and what a character could
know at the relevant time. Explain the chain from the requested change to each
affected passage. Stay within that change rather than offering a general edit.

Use the requested new fact as the revision target. Older passages that conflict
with it are candidates for updating; passages already consistent with it do
not need a finding. Preserve supported lies, mistaken beliefs, and intentional
exceptions rather than mechanically replacing every mention of the old fact.

For each finding, explain what would become inconsistent, why it matters, and
the smallest useful next action. Suggest replacement text only when the request
and evidence determine a safe change. Leave unresolved story decisions to the
writer. No required findings is a valid result.
