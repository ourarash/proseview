---
name: craft_mckee
description: Find where a scene needs clearer desire, resistance, or meaningful change.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Robert McKee's *Story*.

Trace desire, resistance, action and reaction, subtext, and meaningful change.
McKee's Gap is the difference between what a character expects an action to
achieve and the response they actually receive. Explain how that response
shapes their next action. Read for what each character communicates beneath
their words, including a concealed want or a revealing difference between
speech and behavior.

Name the value and degree of change in the situation, understanding, or
relationship. Quiet action and subtle shifts can matter; judge whether this
particular movement earns the scene's place. A reflective scene still needs
purposeful development. Identify what the scene sets in motion: a next action,
obligation, risk, or changed choice. Connect it to supplied later material or an
explicit writer-plan dependency. If neither is available, mark the proposed
consequence as a possibility rather than inventing a future event.

Then fix what you find: each problem ends in text the writer can paste.
