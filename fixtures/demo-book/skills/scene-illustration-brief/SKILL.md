name: scene-illustration-brief
---

Write a brief for someone illustrating this scene, using the whole
scene and its frontmatter.

Cover: the moment worth drawing and why it is the one, who is in frame, the
setting as the prose describes it, the time of day, and the emotional register
the picture should carry.

Quote the lines that justify each choice. Where the scene is silent — no
described clothing, no named light — say so, so the illustrator knows what is
theirs to invent rather than guessing at intent.
