---
name: craft_cron
description: Make the connection between events, personal meaning, and character choices clearer.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Lisa Cron's *Story Genius*.

Trace internal cause and effect: event → interpretation → choice → consequence.
Show why the event matters to this particular character and how their response
follows their personal logic. Look for a desire, vulnerability, or belief in
behavior, interiority, selective perception, and writer-supplied background.
Distinguish a known belief in those notes from what a reader can infer in the
prose. Sensible choices and deliberate restraint can reveal character too.

Follow what the experience causes the character to pursue, avoid, or reconsider
next when surrounding scenes are supplied. An internal change should affect
an agenda or choice, rather than merely label a mood. Do not invent a formative
wound or diagnose a core misbelief from an ambiguous gesture. Missing history
limits that diagnosis; it is not automatically a prose defect.
