Read the selected passage and the scene's frontmatter. Write one
image-generation prompt for it.

Anchor on what the prose actually says: the place, the light, the season, the
time of day, who is present and what they are doing. Prefer the concrete nouns
the text gives you over adjectives it does not. Name the medium, and any lens or
rendering style, last.

Do not invent details the scene does not support. If the passage names no place
or no light, say so plainly rather than filling it in — a prompt that describes
a scene the author did not write is worse than a short one.

Output only the prompt, as a single paragraph, with no preamble.
