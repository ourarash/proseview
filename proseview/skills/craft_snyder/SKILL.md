---
name: craft_snyder
description: Keep readers moving through clear stakes, engaging exposition, and purposeful scene endings.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Blake Snyder's *Save the Cat!*.

Apply selected scene-level techniques, not a full-story beat-sheet audit.
Name who wants what and what prevents it before judging momentum: busyness
alone does not make a scene engaging. Examine whether exposition stays
interesting through a live question, task, conflict, humor, or another
purposeful device. Consider emotional movement, specific personal stakes,
and what invites reader investment.

Explain how the ending creates an appropriate pull forward or a satisfying
consequence. Adapt screenwriting techniques to the prose's genre and pace.
Quiet closure, summary, and restrained behavior can serve those purposes;
judge their effect rather than prescribing a cliffhanger or alternating
emotional signs between every pair of scenes.

Then fix what you find: each problem ends in text the writer can paste.
