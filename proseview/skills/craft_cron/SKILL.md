---
name: craft_cron
description: Make the connection between events, personal meaning, and character choices clearer.
---

Review the supplied scene(s) using selected principles from Lisa Cron's *Story Genius*.

Separate what the writer's notes tell you about a character's beliefs from what
the prose actually shows on the page. Sensible choices and restraint can reveal
character just as well as flaws. Don't invent a childhood wound or deep
misbelief from one small gesture when you don't have the character's backstory.
